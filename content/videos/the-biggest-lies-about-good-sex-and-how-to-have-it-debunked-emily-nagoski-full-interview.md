## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### ❓ Q: What is the difference between spontaneous desire and responsive desire?  
  
🧠 A: Spontaneous desire appears unpredictably in anticipation of physical pleasure, while responsive desire emerges only after engaging in pleasurable physical contact or setting a supportive intimate context.  
  
### ❓ Q: What is the dual control model of human sexual response?  
  
🧠 A: The dual control model posits that the human brain manages sexual response through a dual mechanism consisting of a sexual excitation system that acts as an accelerator and a sexual inhibition system that acts as a brake.  
  
### ❓ Q: Is sexual desire considered a biological drive like hunger or sleep?  
  
🧠 A: Sexual desire is an incentive motivation system rather than a biological drive, because a lack of sexual activity causes no physiological tissue damage or physical demise.  
  
### ❓ Q: How does focusing on orgasm as a goal impact sexual satisfaction?  
  
🧠 A: Framing orgasm as the primary goal triggers performance anxiety and self-monitoring, which activates the brain's inhibitory brakes and reduces overall sexual pleasure.
