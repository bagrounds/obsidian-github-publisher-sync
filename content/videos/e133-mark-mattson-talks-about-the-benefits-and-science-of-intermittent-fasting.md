# 🕒🧬🍎 E133: Mark Mattson talks about the benefits and science of intermittent fasting  
![E133: Mark Mattson talks about the benefits and science of intermittent fasting.](https://youtu.be/WJnWgkpmFSU)  
  
## 🤖 AI Summary  
  
* 🧠 Intermittent fasting is an eating pattern featuring frequent periods with negligible food intake that leverages our evolutionary history of food scarcity.  
* 🧬 Animal studies show that every-other-day food deprivation can extend lifespan by up to 50 percent.  
* 📉 Clinical trials in humans demonstrate that the five-two intermittent fasting approach leads to greater belly fat loss and improved insulin sensitivity compared to traditional continuous calorie restriction.  
* 💀 Modern human brains have decreased in cranial volume by about 10 percent since the agricultural revolution due to disuse of ancient foraging and navigational circuits.  
* 🩻 Obesity correlates with reduced brain size overall and a significantly smaller hippocampus in both adults and adolescents.  
* 🧪 The metabolic switch transitions the body from utilizing liver-derived glucose to burning fat-derived ketones like beta-hydroxybutyrate and acetoacetate.  
* 🧬 Ketones act as signaling molecules that turn on genes encoding brain-derived neurotrophic factor, which supports synaptic plasticity, learning, and memory.  
* 🧹 Fasting for up to 24 hours stimulates cellular autophagy to clear out damaged proteins, free radicals, and dysfunctional mitochondria.  
* 🧘 Intermittent fasting increases GABAergic release onto excitatory neurons in the hippocampus, which reduces anxiety and improves cognitive performance after an initial adjustment period.  
* ⚖️ Clinical trials are actively investigating intermittent fasting for multiple sclerosis, type two diabetes, metabolic syndrome, and Alzheimer disease risk reduction.  
  
## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🥑 Q: What is the metabolic switch during intermittent fasting?  
  
🥑 A: The metabolic switch is the physiological process where glycogen stores in the liver become depleted after roughly twelve hours of fasting, prompting fat cells to release fatty acids that the liver converts into energy-delivering ketones.  
  
### 🧠 Q: How does intermittent fasting protect against neurodegenerative disorders?  
  
🧠 A: Intermittent fasting protects neurons by driving the production of brain-derived neurotrophic factor, enhancing mitochondrial health, decreasing oxidative stress, and dampening neural network hyperexcitability.  
  
### 🏃 Q: Why is physical exercise necessary alongside intermittent fasting?  
  
🏃 A: Regular physical exercise synergizes with intermittent fasting to optimize insulin sensitivity, maintain metabolic health, preserve physical function, and support mental well-being.
