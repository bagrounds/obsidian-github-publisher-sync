## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 📊 Q: What is the primary operational bottleneck for artificial intelligence deployment in enterprise environments according to Vasuman Moza?  
  
📈 A: The primary operational bottleneck is the ability to deeply understand complex business contexts and re-engineer company processes rather than model execution capabilities.  
  
### 💼 Q: Why do enterprise companies reject standalone artificial intelligence solutions that lack system integration?  
  
🏢 A: Enterprise companies have invested millions of dollars and years into established systems of record like NetSuite, Salesforce, SAP, and Dynamics, and they refuse to migrate off them.  
  
### 👥 Q: How do forward deployed engineers map human workflows within a corporate client organization?  
  
🤝 A: Forward deployed engineers sit down directly with departmental process leads across finance, sales, procurement, and logistics to document actual operational steps and failure handling.  
  
### 🤖 Q: What is the purpose of the engagement agent developed by Veric Agents?  
  
💻 A: The engagement agent serves as an assistant for forward deployed engineers to synthesize documentation, process notes, and untangle disparate communications from clients.  
  
### 🧠 Q: Why does Veric Agents fine-tune and post-train open-source models instead of solely relying on frontier models?  
  
📚 A: Frontier models are overly verbose and lack the practical clarity required to filter out trivial details and emphasize what business clients actually care about.
