## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🌱 Q: What creates high-performing cultures?  
🌱 A: High-performing cultures build foundational safety, encourage shared vulnerability, and maintain a clear, commonly understood purpose.  
  
### 🛡️ Q: Why is safety important for teams?  
🛡️ A: Psychological safety allows individuals to experiment, fail, and learn without fear, which accelerates creativity and group problem-solving.  
  
### 🗣️ Q: How do leaders build trust?  
🗣️ A: Leaders build trust by modeling vulnerability, such as admitting their own mistakes, which invites others to engage honestly.
