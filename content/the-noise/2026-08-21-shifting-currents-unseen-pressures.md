🇺🇸 **California Wildfires Rage On**. 🔥 Multiple large wildfires continue to burn across California, exacerbated by high temperatures, strong winds, and prolonged drought conditions, forcing thousands of evacuations, as reported by the Los Angeles Times on Thursday. 💨 Air quality alerts remain in effect for vast areas.  
🇪🇺 **Europe Battles Persistent Drought and Heat**. 🌡️ Southern and central Europe are experiencing another week of intense heatwaves and deepening drought, severely impacting agriculture and water reserves, according to a BBC Weather report on Friday. 💧 Several rivers, including parts of the Po and the Loire, are at alarmingly low levels.  
🇮🇳 **Monsoon Flooding in Northern India**. ⛈️ Heavy monsoon rains have caused severe flooding and landslides in parts of northern India, leading to fatalities and widespread infrastructure damage, an Al Jazeera report confirmed on Thursday. 💔 Rescue operations are underway in affected regions.  
🇦🇺 **Great Barrier Reef Bleaching Event**. 🌊 Scientists confirmed a new mass coral bleaching event on Australia's Great Barrier Reef, linked to elevated ocean temperatures, raising further concerns about the reef's long-term survival, The Guardian reported on Friday.  
🌎 **New Report on Global Methane Emissions**. 🏭 A new UN Environment Programme report highlighted persistent challenges in reducing global methane emissions from agriculture and fossil fuel industries, calling for more aggressive policy measures, as detailed by NPR on Thursday.

🇬🇧 **NHS Waiting Lists Remain Long**. 🏥 The UK's National Health Service (NHS) announced that waiting lists for routine treatments remain historically long, despite efforts to clear backlogs, sparking renewed debate over healthcare funding, The Guardian reported on Friday.  
🌍 **Global Polio Eradication Efforts Face Setbacks**. 🦠 Efforts to eradicate polio globally have encountered new setbacks, with recent outbreaks reported in several African nations, underscoring the challenges of vaccine distribution and hesitancy, according to the World Health Organization on Wednesday.  
🇺🇸 **Youth Mental Health Crisis Continues**. 🫂 A new study published by the American Academy of Pediatrics highlighted the ongoing rise in mental health challenges among adolescents in the US, calling for increased access to mental health services in schools, as reported by NPR this week.

🇺🇸 **Presidential Debates Preparations Underway**. 🎤 Both major US presidential campaigns are actively preparing for the first series of debates scheduled for early next month, with strategy and messaging being fine-tuned, The New York Times reported on Thursday. 📊 Polls indicate a tight race in several key swing states.  
🇺🇸 **Congressional Budget Negotiations Begin**. 🏛️ Negotiations for the upcoming fiscal year's federal budget have begun in Congress, with sharp divisions expected over spending priorities and the national debt, a Washington Post analysis noted on Friday.  
🇺🇸 **Supreme Court to Hear Major Privacy Case**. ⚖️ The US Supreme Court announced it would hear a landmark case concerning digital privacy rights and government access to personal data, setting the stage for a significant ruling on evolving technologies, as reported by the Associated Press on Thursday.
