## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🤔 Q: What is the pardon-proof method Congress can use to enforce subpoenas?  
  
⚖️ A: Congress can petition a federal court for civil contempt, asking a judge to overrule invalid privilege claims and impose coercive daily fines or up to 18 months in jail until the witness complies.  
  
### 🛡️ Q: Why can a presidential pardon not stop civil contempt or civil lawsuits?  
  
🌐 A: Federal pardon power applies only to federal criminal offenses and cannot reach civil court orders, coercive civil contempt detention, state crimes, or private civil liability such as disgorgement and tax claims.  
  
### 📋 Q: How does civil contempt differ from criminal contempt when someone defies a subpoena?  
  
🔍 A: Criminal contempt punishes a completed act of defiance through Department of Justice prosecution and cannot be undone by later cooperation, whereas civil contempt uses fines or jail coercively and ends immediately once the witness complies.  
  
### 🏠 Q: Why did Elise Stefanik's home sale draw scrutiny as an example for investigators?  
  
🕵️ A: Elise Stefanik reportedly sold a property for 900,000 dollars above market value to an undisclosed buyer without offering a public explanation, a pattern investigators treat as a red flag for potential bribery or money laundering.  
  
### 🖥️ Q: What corporate and national security issues were raised regarding Nvidia?  
  
🚨 A: Nvidia faced criticism over reinstating an executive linked to alleged secret chip sales to China while lacking a corporate compliance officer and maintaining financial and political ties with Donald Trump.
