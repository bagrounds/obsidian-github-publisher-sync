## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🗂️ Q: What does scientific data reveal about introducing common food allergens to infants?  
  
🗂️ A: Research proves that introducing allergens like peanuts early between four and six months old results in a dramatic reduction in peanut allergy rates.  
  
### 🗂️ Q: Does choosing to formula feed instead of breastfeed negatively impact infant attachment?  
  
🗂️ A: Evidence confirms that alternative feeding methods do not impair secure attachment, and parental mental well-being takes priority over rigid feeding pressures.  
  
### 🗂️ Q: What do developmental milestone guidelines indicate about infant walking ages?  
  
🗂️ A: Pediatric guidelines establish wide normal ranges for physical milestones, showing that healthy children can learn to walk anywhere between seven and eighteen months of age.  
  
### 🗂️ Q: How does infant sleep training impact long-term child development?  
  
🗂️ A: Controlled studies demonstrate that sleep training effectively helps infants connect sleep cycles and improves family rest without causing short-term or long-term harm.
