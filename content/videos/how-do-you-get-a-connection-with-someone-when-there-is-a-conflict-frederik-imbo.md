# 🤝💥💬 How do you get a connection with someone when there is a conflict? - Frederik Imbo  
![How do you get a connection with someone when there is a conflict? - Frederik Imbo](https://youtu.be/NEivTMn5Tgw)  
  
## 🤖 AI Summary  
  
* 🗣️ Raging with mean attacks during a conflict comes from the jackal part of us that judges, accuses, and wants to be right.  
* ⚡ Outbursts driven by the jackal increase tension and lead to electrocution rather than connection.  
* 🦒 Reconnecting with someone requires getting in touch with your inner giraffe, which focuses on repairing contact and obtaining connection.  
* 👂 Giraffes listen with empathetic ears to hear unfulfilled needs rather than taking statements as personal attacks.  
* 💬 Giraffes honestly express what is alive in them by sharing their feelings and needs.  
* 🛣️ Communication can move smoothly through a four step roundabout to avoid traffic jams in conversation.  
* 📝 Step one requires describing a clear, factual, neutral observation that triggered the dissatisfaction.  
* 💭 Step two involves stating the specific feeling experienced, such as feeling frustrated or dispirited.  
* 🎯 Step three articulates the underlying unfulfilled need, such as needing clarity.  
* ❓ Step four concludes by making a concrete, actionable request to the other person.  
* 🔄 Connecting requires paying attention to what is alive in the other person by empathically listening to their observation, feelings, needs, and requests.  
* 🎭 The four steps do not need to be used in a rigid or artificial order during real conversations.  
* 🌟 Bringing up your inner giraffe increases opportunities for real contact, mutual understanding, and durable conflict resolution.

### 🦊 Q: What represents the part of human nature that judges, accuses, and triggers conflict during arguments?  
  
🦁 A: The jackal symbolizes the reactive part of human nature that attacks, demands to be right, and escalates tension.  
  
### 🦒 Q: What archetype symbolizes the desire to listen, repair contact, and achieve authentic connection during disputes?  
  
🫀 A: The giraffe represents the inner mindset focused on empathy, unfulfilled needs, and constructive mutual understanding.  
  
### 🛣️ Q: What four step communication framework helps individuals express observations, feelings, needs, and requests smoothly?  
  
🚗 A: The roundabout framework guides speakers through a neutral observation, personal feeling, underlying need, and concrete request.
