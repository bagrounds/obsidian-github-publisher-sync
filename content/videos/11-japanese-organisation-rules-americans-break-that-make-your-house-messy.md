## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🚪 Q: How do Japanese entryways prevent clutter from spreading into living spaces?  
  
A: A dedicated entryway zone called a genkan stops shoes, coats, and bags right at the front door before they reach household surfaces.  
  
### 🗄️ Q: Why are flat horizontal stacks considered ineffective for home organization?  
  
A: Horizontal storage hides items at the bottom, making them invisible and requiring an unwanted decision to unmake a pile every time.  
  
### 📦 Q: What is the primary cause of overflowing junk drawers in homes?  
  
A: Undivided compartments lack specific addresses for items, causing miscellaneous objects to naturally drift into a messy pile over time.
