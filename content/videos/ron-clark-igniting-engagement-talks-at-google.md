## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🤝 Q: What is the Amazing Shake competition created by Ron Clark?  
  
👔 A: The Amazing Shake is a global program and competition designed to teach young students essential professional etiquette, public speaking, poise, and interpersonal skills through real-world scenarios judged by community and business leaders.  
  
### 💼 Q: How does the Ron Clark Academy fund its educational scholarships?  
  
🏢 A: The Ron Clark Academy funds scholarships by generating revenue through multiple internal business ventures, including a custom merchandise printing warehouse, a proprietary educational software app, facility event rentals, and professional development training for visiting educators.  
  
### 🤖 Q: How does Ron Clark integrate artificial intelligence into classroom instruction?  
  
🎙️ A: Ron Clark encourages educators to lean into artificial intelligence by teaching students how to critically evaluate AI-generated writing and using tools like NotebookLM to convert historical research into custom audio podcasts.
