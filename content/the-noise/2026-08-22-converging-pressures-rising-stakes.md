### 💥 Geopolitical Flashpoints and Unfolding Conflicts  
  
🇺🇸🇮🇷 **US Intensifies Economic Pressure on Iran**. 💬 The United States has launched what President Trump's administration calls an "Economic D-Day" against Iran, threatening unprecedented isolation measures and reportedly conducting a covert operation to open oil shipping lanes in the Strait of Hormuz, as detailed by Just Security and CCFGroup. 🚢 Iran's foreign ministry has rejected these claims, asserting the Strait remains its territory, while Malaysia noted it would be affected by disruptions.  
🇺🇦🇷🇺 **Ukraine War: Kyiv Attacked, Russia's Fuel Shortages**. 💔 Russia launched a massive attack on Kyiv, killing at least 17 people and prompting concerns over Ukraine's dwindling interceptor missile supplies, according to a WORLD report. 💥 Meanwhile, Russia is reportedly suffering fuel shortages due to recent Ukrainian strikes, as the Foreign Office News Round-Up noted.  
🇮🇱🇵🇸 **Palestinian Teen Killed in West Bank**. 🚨 A 17-year-old Palestinian was killed by Israeli settler gunfire in Sa'ir, north of Hebron, following a rock-throwing incident, the Associated Press reported. 🇹🇷 Turkey is seeking an international warrant for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's arrest over a past Gaza naval blockade incident.  
🇱🇧 **Lebanon's Economy Derails Amidst Conflict**. 📉 Lebanon's economy is projected to contract by 6.4% in 2026, as renewed conflict reversed a fragile stabilization from the previous year, according to the World Bank.  
🇫🇮🇷🇺 **Finland Reports Russian Airspace Violation**. 🛡️ The Finnish Defense Ministry is investigating a suspected violation of its airspace by a Russian aircraft over the western Gulf of Finland, following an uptick in Russian military flights near NATO's eastern border, the Foreign Office News Round-Up reported.  
🇧🇼🇷🇺 **Botswana Foils Russian Military Recruitment**. 🚫 Botswana's International Relations Minister informed parliament that eight citizens were intercepted at various airports while attempting to join the Russian military for deployment in Ukraine, as OkayAfrica reported.  
🇱🇷🇺🇸 **US Deportees Arrive in Liberia**. 🤝 Twenty migrants deported from the United States have arrived in Liberia, marking the first group of up to 1,200 people the West African nation will receive under a controversial new agreement, Africanews reported.  
🇹🇿 **Tanzanian Opposition Leader Faces Treason Trial**. ⚖️ Tanzania's opposition leader, Tundu Lissu, is set to face trial on treason charges after a court ruled he has a case to answer for allegedly inciting a public boycott of November elections, Africanews stated.  
🇨🇦🇩🇴🇺🇸 **US Imports Dominican Sugar Despite Forced Labor Concerns**. 🏭 The New York Times reported that the US is importing sugar from a major Dominican producer with ties to President Trump, despite evidence of forced labor conditions at one of its plantations.  
  
### 💰 Economic Currents and Market Shifts  
  
🇺🇸 **US Markets See Mixed Signals**. 📊 US stocks rose on Friday, trimming some weekly losses, amidst rekindled inflation concerns and a jumpy bond market, as reported by KVUE. 📈 While strong US business activity was noted by Interactive Brokers, the Treasury's bond buyback rally fizzled, and a cooling in the US economic surprise index was observed by decorahnews.com.  
🇯🇵 **Japan's Inflation Rises, Manufacturing Strong**. 📈 Japan's July CPI inflation rose to 1.9% year-on-year, and the preliminary August Manufacturing PMI rebounded, signaling strong health in the sector, XTB reported.  
🇬🇧 **UK Retail Sales Dip, Services Sector Grows**. 📉 UK retail sales came in below forecasts, dropping 0.5% monthly, but the services sector's PMI jumped to a 20-month high, offsetting a fall in manufacturing PMI, according to XTB and Interactive Brokers.  
🇰🇷 **Hyundai Motor Union Launches Full-Scale Strike**. 🚗 Hyundai Motor's labor union initiated a full-scale strike on Friday, its first in a decade, after wage and collective bargaining talks broke down, THE AsiaN reported.  
🇺🇸 **Walmart Stock Tumbles on Outlook**. 📉 Walmart's stock closed about 9% lower Thursday, as Wall Street appeared disappointed by the retailer's quarterly comparable sales and sales guidance, despite beating overall sales estimates, Go Local Prov reported.  
🇺🇸 **Washington State Invests in Manufacturing**. 💰 The Washington State Department of Commerce announced $1 million in grants to seven businesses and organizations to accelerate manufacturing expansion and workforce development.

🇰🇷🇪🇺 **South Korea Partners on Semiconductor Research**. 💡 Seoul has launched an initiative to partner with European and Asian universities and firms to develop cutting-edge chip materials and fabrication methods, according to a Facebook update.
