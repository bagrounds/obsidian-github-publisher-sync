## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### ❓ What is the main premise of What Got You Here Won't Get You There?  
  
🧠 Executive coach Marshall Goldsmith demonstrates that the technical skills and behavioral habits that propel individuals to initial career success often become the exact obstacles preventing them from reaching higher executive levels.  
  
### ❓ What is feedforward and how does it differ from feedback?  
  
🔮 Feedforward is a developmental process where an individual selects a targeted behavior to improve, describes the objective to a colleague, receives two actionable suggestions for future improvement, and accepts the advice with a simple thank you without defending past actions.  
  
### ❓ What is the success delusion described by Marshall Goldsmith?  
  
DEL Leaders suffering from the success delusion attribute all of their achievements to their current behavioral habits, failing to realize that they often achieved success in spite of certain toxic behaviors rather than because of them.  
  
### ❓ Why do successful leaders struggle with listening?  
  
🙉 Successful leaders frequently fall prey to habits like winning too much or adding too much value, which creates an internal urge to speak, judge, or correct others rather than absorb external input.
