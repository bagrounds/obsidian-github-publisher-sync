## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🌍 Q: Why do people struggle to express their true feelings and needs during everyday conversations?  
  
🌐 A: People struggle because they are often out of touch with their internal emotional states and fear that opening up will lead to punitive or defensive reactions.  
  
### 🧩 Q: How does taking a comment personally indicate an internal issue rather than an external fault?  
  
🧩 A: Taking offense signals that an unfulfilled need or past emotional pain has been triggered within the listener, requiring self-reflection instead of blame.  
  
### 🎤 Q: What is the primary mental shift required to overcome public speaking anxiety?  
  
🎤 A: Overcoming anxiety requires changing the internal belief that the audience is judging you to believing that the audience wants to connect and listen.
