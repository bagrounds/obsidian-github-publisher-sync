### 🗳️ Q: How does the election process for the Speaker of the House differ from the Senate Majority Leader?  
  
🤖 A: The Speaker of the House is elected by a vote of the full House of Representatives, requiring candidate consensus across the entire chamber. The Senate Majority Leader is chosen strictly by a vote within the majority political party caucus.  
  
### 📜 Q: Why was the Electoral College originally created during the Constitutional Convention?  
  
🤖 A: The framers of the Constitution created the Electoral College as an independent selection body to prevent national military organizations or congressional factions from improperly controlling executive elections.  
  
### 🏢 Q: Why was the total number of representatives in the United States House capped in 1929?  
  
🤖 A: Congress capped the total number of seats in the House of Representatives in 1929 to restrict the growing legislative voting power of rapidly expanding urban populations and immigrant communities.  
  
### 📜 Q: How did Montana address corporate money in politics during the 2026 midterm election?
