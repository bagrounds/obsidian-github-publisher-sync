# 🪜🆙📈 What Got you Here Won't Get you there - Marshall Goldsmith (Book Summary)  
![What Got you Here Won't Get you there - Marshall Goldsmith (Book Summary)](https://youtu.be/dt95ma33abY)  
  
## 🎯🤖 AI Summary  
  
* 🧠 Behavioral flaws rather than technical incompetence prevent successful people from reaching the next level.  
* 🗣️ Adding value is a common trap where leaders inject their opinion into a team member's idea, inadvertently destroying the creator's sense of ownership.  
* 🏆 Winning too much drives an obsessive need to win in all situations, even when the issue is trivial and the cost outweighs any potential benefit.  
* 🛑 Reflecting on personal habits requires identifying what behaviors to stop rather than what new tasks to start.  
* 🤝 Saying thank you is a simple, powerful mechanism to acknowledge others without complicating interactions or sparking defensiveness.  
  
## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 📌 Q: What is the main premise of Marshall Goldsmith's teachings on professional growth?  
  
📌 A: Technical skills and intelligence successfully elevate individuals to senior positions, but interpersonal behavioral flaws obstruct further advancement.  
  
### 📌 Q: Why does adding value to someone else's idea backfire?  
  
📌 A: Injecting personal modifications into an idea diminishes the original creator's sense of ownership and reduces their commitment to execution.  
  
### 📌 Q: What defines the winning too much tendency?  
  
📌 A: The compulsive desire to win every argument and prove superiority regardless of whether the outcome matters.
