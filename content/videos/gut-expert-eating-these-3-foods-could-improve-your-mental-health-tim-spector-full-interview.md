## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### ❓ Q: How does the gut microbiome directly influence mental health?  
  
A: The gut microbiome communicates with the brain along the vagus nerve using short-chain fatty acids, neurotransmitters, and hormones, meaning gut inflammation can signal the brain to trigger depressive symptoms and fatigue.  
  
### ❓ Q: Why is calorie counting considered an outdated approach to nutrition?  
  
A: Calorie counting ignores the structural quality of whole foods and chemical complexity needed to feed gut microbes, leading to hunger signals that eventually disrupt long-term diet attempts.  
  
### ❓ Q: What are the easiest ways to increase plant diversity in a weekly diet?  
  
A: Plant diversity expands rapidly by counting nuts, seeds, herbs, spices, coffee, and legumes alongside standard fruits and vegetables toward a weekly goal of 30 distinct plants.  
  
### ❓ Q: How do oral hygiene habits impact long-term brain and heart health?  
  
A: Flossing and brushing remove pro-inflammatory bacterial plaque in the mouth, preventing oral microbes from triggering chronic inflammation linked to heart disease and cognitive decline.
