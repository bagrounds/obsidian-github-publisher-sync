### 🔬 Advancing Health & Scientific Frontiers  
  
🧬 Researchers at MIT and collaborating institutions have unveiled over 600 new tissue models of human cancer, derived from patient tumors, which will significantly aid in the development of targeted cancer treatments. 💊 An experimental drug developed by researchers at The University of Texas at Austin is showing remarkable effectiveness against aggressive melanoma in mice by uniquely exploiting cancer cells' sugar appetite while cutting off their fat supply. 💉 The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has granted accelerated approval to Tudriqev, a genetically modified oncolytic viral therapy, in combination with nivolumab for advanced, refractory melanoma patients who have progressed on prior PD-1-blocking antibody-based regimens. 🧠 A Phase 3 clinical trial led by Weill Cornell Medicine and partners revealed that an earlier use of targeted radionuclide therapy significantly delays metastatic prostate cancer progression, potentially changing the standard of care for many patients. 🧬 Three gene therapies originating from the NIH Intramural Research Program have made Genetic Engineering & Biotechnology News (GEN) Top 10 Best Sellers list, highlighting their impact in patient care. 💡 Scientists have generated quantum entanglement directly from sunlight, a breakthrough that could offer a lower-energy alternative to the lasers typically used in quantum technology, as reported by ScienceDaily on Friday. 🔭 NASA astronauts Jessica Meir and Anil Menon successfully completed a spacewalk to prepare the International Space Station's 3B power channel for the future installation of new roll-out solar arrays, enhancing the station's power capabilities.  
  
### 🌿 Greening Our Planet & Powering Progress  
  
⚡ New Mexico's Energy Transition Act has successfully bolstered clean energy, shifting the state's electricity supply to a majority of renewables in just five years, according to the League of Conservation Voters. 🏙️ New York has adopted updated regulations for the Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative (RGGI), lowering the cap on climate emissions from the power sector, with projections to reduce emissions by 89% below 2024 levels by 2037. 📜 A federal appeals court upheld an injunction barring the EPA from freezing $20 billion in grants from the Inflation Reduction Act's Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund, ensuring critical funding for greenhouse gas reduction projects. 🌳 In a significant conservation win, the Land Conservancy of San Luis Obispo County and San Luis Outdoor Painters for the Environment (SLOPE) are hosting an art show to raise funds for protecting local lands, having already conserved over 68,000 acres. 🐢 New Zealand's kākāpō, one of the world's rarest birds, achieved its most successful breeding season on record in 2026 with over 100 chicks hatched, demonstrating effective conservation efforts. 🐍 The 2026 Florida Python Challenge saw over 900 participants remove 280 Burmese pythons from the Everglades, contributing significantly to native wildlife conservation. 🌿 Senator Saud Anwar of Connecticut was named an Environmental Champion by the Connecticut League of Conservation Voters for his perfect 2026 legislative record supporting environmental rights and solar programs. 🌊 More than 10% of the global ocean is now officially under protection, marking a historic benchmark for marine conservation efforts, with Ghana establishing its first national marine protected area.  
  
### 💻 Technology & Education for Empowerment  
  
📚 An online Summer Skills Boost Program in Arlington attracted almost 2,000 students, providing personalized educational pathways and preventing learning loss for the upcoming school year. 🎓 Illinois community colleges are creating one of the largest interactive education exhibits at the 2026 Illinois State Fair, offering hands-on career demonstrations and highlighting pathways to high-demand jobs. 🤝 The 2026 Bolder Futures Fellowship, sponsored by Micron, is bridging the gap between emerging AI technology and the social sector by providing AI training and mentoring for early career professionals and nonprofit partners. 💡 The AGI Conference 2026 will feature a dedicated session on Artificial General Intelligence for social good, exploring its potential to revolutionize healthcare, climate change, education, and social equity. 🤖 The AI for SDGs Global Youth AI Future Innovation Competition 2026 is providing a global platform for young innovators to develop and scale AI solutions that transform education and advance sustainable development goals. 📈 A free online AI information session for nonprofits on August 7th demonstrated how AI can enhance data analysis, personalized communication, grant research, and overall operational efficiency for charitable organizations.  
  
### 🤝 Community & Global Cooperation Flourish
