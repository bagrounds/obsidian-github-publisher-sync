### 🔬 Pioneering Health & Scientific Horizons  
  
💉 Duke University researchers have developed an injectable scaffold that helped stroke-damaged brains grow new blood vessels, support nerve regrowth, and recover movement in mice. 🧠 UCLA researchers have uncovered two powerful influences that guide how the human brain forms before birth, involving radial glia stem cells. 💊 Ionis Pharmaceuticals has received FDA approval for ZANVASTRO™ (zilganersen), the first and only disease-modifying treatment for Alexander disease, an ultra-rare neurological disorder. 🌍 Ten African countries have signed a Cross-Border Collaboration Memorandum of Understanding to strengthen coordination, information-sharing, and joint response to public health emergencies. 🦠 The World Health Organization has allocated 70,000 doses of the Ervebo vaccine to Congo, with 20,000 earmarked for a late-stage trial to determine its effectiveness against the Bundibugyo virus. 💡 Scientists have identified a shared biological mechanism explaining chronic exhaustion in conditions like long COVID and PTSD, revealing striking biological similarities between previously separate disorders. 🔬 Carnegie Mellon University scientists have discovered an unexpected form of the Hall effect, overturning a century-old physics assumption about this electrical response. 🧬 Researchers have found that over 500 species of vesper bats possess two separate sets of antibody genes, an immune arrangement never before observed in mammals. ⚛️ NANO Nuclear Energy and Howden are advancing the engineering collaboration for the KRONOS MMR™ Primary Helium Circulator to the detailed design phase, a critical component for clean energy micro modular reactors.  
  
### 🌿 Greening Our World & Powering Progress

Grant is leading Oxfam's Second Hand September campaign, encouraging Britons to buy pre-loved clothes and reduce textile waste. 🦁 The DSC Foundation Gala united global conservationists and honored Steve Hornady, supporting initiatives like the Tanzania Lion Illumination Project, which protects livestock enclosures. 🌲 The Wild Sheep Society of British Columbia has restored 900 acres of habitat for Stone's sheep through targeted prescribed fire treatments. 🏞️ Public land closures in Baker, Oregon for the Fox Fire have been partially rescinded, reopening Basser Diggins Campground and a day-use area.
