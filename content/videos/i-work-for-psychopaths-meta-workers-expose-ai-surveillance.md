# 👿👁️💻 “I Work For Psychopaths”: Meta Workers Expose AI Surveillance  
![“I Work For Psychopaths”: Meta Workers Expose AI Surveillance](https://youtu.be/ca7ufSI1Qxg)  
  
## 🤖 AI Summary  
  
* 💻 Tech workers inside big tech companies feel disillusioned by workplace surveillance and leadership goals to replace human staff with AI agents \[[00:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=6)].  
* 👁️ About two-thirds of US workers face surveillance from bossware, a tracking technology rooted in Henry Ford era assembly lines that is now supercharged by AI \[[01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=62)].  
* 🏥 Triage nurses at Kaiser Permanente face strict 13-minute call targets and disciplinary threats, forcing them to rush and risking unsafe patient care \[[02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=130)].  
* 🚚 Blue-collar and service workers endure extreme algorithmic control, including automated warehouse firings, yawn-detecting truck cameras, and AI earpieces dictating restroom cleanings at Burger King \[[02:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=177)].  
* 🎭 Emotion AI systems judge call center workers like Sherry Shaw on vocal warmth and empathy, demanding human employees act like robots \[[03:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=200)].  
* 📉 Self-surveillance with remote bossware reveals how opaque algorithms misclassify legitimate research as unproductive time and generate arbitrary productivity scores \[[03:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=234)].  
* ⚖️ A lawsuit against Meta alleges that AI-assisted performance tools penalized employees who took medical or parental leave, leading to unfair inclusion in mass layoffs \[[05:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=350)].  
* 🖱️ Meta launched the Model Capability Initiative to record employee screens, keystrokes, and mouse clicks to train AI replacements before employee petitions forced a pause \[[06:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=418)].  
* 💰 Bossware vendors and bankrupt companies monetize workplace data as surveillance sludge, selling employee recordings and corporate records to data brokers or tech giants like Google to train AI models \[[09:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=556)].  
* 🏛️ California passed laws banning robo-boss firings and restricting emotion and brain-data monitoring, though heavy tech lobbying stalled bills banning AI replacement training \[[11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=687)].  
* ✊ Union organizing and collective worker petitions remain vital defenses against executive power consolidation and invasive workplace surveillance \[[12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=754)].  
  
## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🕵️ Q: What is workplace bossware and how does artificial intelligence change employee monitoring?  
  
💻 A: Workplace bossware refers to digital monitoring software that tracks employee activity, and artificial intelligence upgrades these tools by analyzing keystrokes, vocal tone, facial expressions, and productivity scores to automate evaluations and train replacement AI agents \[[01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=65)], \[[03:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=200)], \[[06:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=418)].  
  
### 🧠 Q: How did Meta use internal employee data under the Model Capability Initiative?  
  
⌨️ A: Meta introduced the Model Capability Initiative to capture internal staff screen recordings, mouse clicks, and keystrokes so artificial intelligence models could learn how high-performing workers operate computers, though Meta paused the effort after more than 1,800 workers signed a protest petition \[[06:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=418)], \[[08:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=523)].  
  
### 📜 Q: What legal protections exist against AI workplace surveillance in California?  
  
⚖️ A: California enacted legislation prohibiting automated algorithms from independently hiring or firing employees and restricting the use of emotion-tracking AI and neural brain-data monitoring in the workplace \[[11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=701)].  
  
### 💼 Q: What happens to employee surveillance data collected by third-party monitoring vendors?  
  
📊 A: Third-party bossware companies frequently collect excessive workplace data known as surveillance sludge, which vendors can sell to advertisers and data brokers or use to train commercial AI customer service agents \[[09:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=556)], \[[10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ca7ufSI1Qxg&t=625)].

* 🌐 The Age of Surveillance Capitalism: The Fight for a Human Future at the New Frontier of Power by Shoshana Zuboff details how technology corporations extract behavioral data to predict, control, and monetize human activity.  
  
### 🆚 Contrasting

* ⚙️ The Principles of Scientific Management by Frederick Winslow Taylor advocates for rigorous time-motion measurement and standardized workplace efficiency as mutually beneficial drivers of industrial productivity and prosperity.
