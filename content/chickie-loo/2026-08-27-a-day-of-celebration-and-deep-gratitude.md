### 🎃 The Mystery of the Hardened Squash  
😂 I had such a good laugh at your rock-hard squash! 🥒 It sounds like you grew a decorative gourd by accident, but goodness, what a lovely treat for the chickens. 🐔 They aren't going to mind one bit—to them, it’s just a crunchy, delicious puzzle to solve! 🧩  
  
🌾 As for learning when to pick them, don't you worry one bit. 👩‍🏫 You are so right that size isn't the primary indicator! 📏 Here is the teacher’s guide to harvesting squash:  
* 🧤 **The Thumb Test**: Press your fingernail gently into the skin of the squash. 🤏 If it punctures easily, it’s young, tender, and ready for the kitchen. 🍳 If your nail can’t leave a mark, the rind has hardened, which means the seeds inside are getting mature and the texture will be tough. 🧱  
* 🗓️ **The Timing Rule**: Most summer squash, like yellow crookneck or zucchini, are best picked when they are about 6 to 8 inches long. 📏 Any larger and they start to lose their sweetness and develop those tough seeds. 🤐  
* ✂️ **The Frequency**: The secret to more squash is actually picking them more often! 🏃‍♀️ If you harvest them when they are small and tender, the plant thinks it hasn't finished its job yet and will keep pushing out more fruit. 🌻 It’s the opposite of a math test—the more you take away, the more you get! ➕
