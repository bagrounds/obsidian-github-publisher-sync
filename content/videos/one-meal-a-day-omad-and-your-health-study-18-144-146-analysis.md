# 🍽️📅🩺 One Meal a Day (OMAD) and your Health [Study 18 144-146 Analysis]

## 🍽️ AI Summary  
  
- 🍽️ One meal a day is an intermittent fasting approach where all daily energy intake is consumed in a single feeding window.  
- 👥 Study 18 investigated fifteen healthy normal weight adults randomized to one meal a day versus three meals a day across eight weeks.  
- 📈 Total and low density lipoprotein cholesterol increased significantly during the eight week one meal a day protocol.  
- 🩸 Oral glucose tolerance tests revealed reduced glucose tolerance and lower first phase insulin release after one meal a day.  
- ⚖️ Eleven day trials demonstrated that one meal a day caused greater reductions in body weight and fat mass than three meals a day.  
- ⏳ Ten week trials involving overweight adults showed that four hour and six hour feeding windows produced equivalent weight loss of 3.2 percent body weight.  
- 📉 Fasting longer does not consistently yield greater weight loss among individuals practicing time restricted feeding.  
- 📉 One meal a day reliably encourages unintended weight loss without explicit calorie counting or restriction.  
- 📊 Data regarding blood pressure shifts and glycemic control remains mixed across clinical studies indicating requirements for further research.  
- 🔬 Future studies are necessary to make firm conclusions regarding the physiological impacts of intermittent fasting protocols.  
  
## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🥑 Q: What characterizes a one meal a day intermittent fasting regimen?  
  
🥑 A: One meal a day is an intermittent fasting protocol where individuals consume their entire daily energy intake and all macronutrients within a single feeding window lasting one to two hours.  
  
### ⚖️ Q: Does following a one meal a day diet cause weight loss without deliberate calorie restriction?  
  
⚖️ A: One meal a day reliably promotes unintended weight loss because restricting eating to a single daily window naturally reduces overall caloric intake without explicit tracking.  
  
### 💓 Q: How does a one meal a day diet impact blood cholesterol levels according to clinical trials?  
  
💓 A: Clinical evaluations demonstrate that maintaining weight on a one meal a day diet leads to notable increases in total cholesterol and low density lipoprotein cholesterol levels.  
  
### 🩸 Q: What do oral glucose tolerance tests reveal about metabolic responses to one meal a day diets?  
  
🩸 A: Oral glucose tolerance tests indicate that individuals following a one meal a day protocol experience reduced glucose tolerance and a dampened initial phase of insulin release.
