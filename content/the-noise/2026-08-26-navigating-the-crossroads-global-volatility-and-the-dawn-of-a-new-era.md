However, consumer confidence edged lower in August as expectations weakened, despite an improvement in the assessment of current conditions. Experts warn that Europe still lacks sufficient backup capacity to cover prolonged periods of low renewable energy output. In a move reflecting this trend, Uniper and Equinor recently signed a 15-year agreement for natural gas supply to Germany.

### 🥵 Climate's Relentless Assault  
  
🌍 **Global Heatwaves and Extreme Weather Persist**. 🌡️ The first half of 2026 saw record-breaking heatwaves across Europe, Asia, Africa, and North America, with 2026 projected to be the second-warmest year on record globally, averaging around 1.51°C warmer than pre-industrial times. These conditions fueled exceptional wildfires across Western Europe, which were the fourth worst on record by early August. Smoke haze from Canadian blazes impacted large swathes of the US, while Indonesia faces its biggest wildfires since the 2015 El Niño, and India contends with its weakest monsoon in two decades.  
  
💧 **Intensifying Drought and El Niño Concerns**. 🌀 Drought continues to impact Europe, with low water levels affecting the Rhine and Danube rivers and reducing nuclear power plant output in countries like Germany and Hungary. The World Meteorological Organization predicts that the intensifying El Niño phenomenon will dominate global climate patterns from August to October 2026, increasing the likelihood of above-normal temperatures worldwide and significant shifts in rainfall patterns. This strong El Niño also raises concerns about climate change-intensified diseases like cholera and dengue, particularly where extreme weather intersects with existing disease transmission.
