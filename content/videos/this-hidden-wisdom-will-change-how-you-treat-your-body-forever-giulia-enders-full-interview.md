## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🫁 Q: How does the lung's physical structure influence daily energy consumption?  
  
🫁 A: The lung maintains a soft, passive, and accommodating structure that allows it to expand easily within the chest cavity. This flexible design ensures the body can complete over 20,000 daily breaths with minimal caloric effort, preventing the excessive energy expenditure associated with rigid or inflamed lung tissue.  
  
### 🩸 Q: Why does blood iron drop temporarily following an infection?  
  
🩸 A: During an infection, the immune system intentionally removes iron from the bloodstream and sequesters it in hidden storage sites. Because harmful bacteria rely on host iron to multiply, this mechanism starves invading pathogens until the immune response resolves the threat.  
  
### 🪢 Q: What is interpersonal synchronization and how is it measured?  
  
🪢 A: Interpersonal synchronization is a physiological phenomenon where two individuals in close physical contact align their autonomic responses. When a calm individual holds hands with a stressed person, the stressed partner shows lowered skin conductivity, decreased cortisol levels, and a heart rate that shifts into harmony with the calmer person.  
  
### 🦾 Q: What role does the protein titin play in muscle mechanics?  
  
🦾 A: Titin is a giant elastic muscle protein that acts like a coiled spring. It absorbs kinetic energy generated by external resistance or impact and re-releases that stored energy during contraction, giving movements an extra elastic power boost in activities like jumping or yoga.  
  
### 🧠 Q: How does the complexity of the human immune system compare to artificial intelligence?  
  
🧠 A: The human immune system operates through trillions of living cellular connection points that constantly exchange signals and map biological patterns. In contrast, modern artificial intelligence models utilize roughly 150 million data connections, making organic cellular networks vastly more complex and integrated than current AI systems.
