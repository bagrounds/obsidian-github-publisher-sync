[cite: KuCoin]

🤖🏛️ **White House Expands AI Safety Framework**. 🏛️ The Trump administration moved to extend its voluntary AI safety framework to encompass open-weight AI models, aiming for a 30-day pre-release government review for frontier-level capabilities. 🚗🛣️ **Nevada Approves Thousands of Robotaxis**. 🛣️ The Nevada Transportation Authority granted commercial permits for up to 8,000 robotaxis from companies like Tesla, Waymo, and Uber to operate in Clark County. [cite: Tesla Oracle]  
  
🌌✨ **Hubble and Webb Capture Stunning Black Eye Galaxy Image**. ✨ A composite image combining data from the James Webb and Hubble Space Telescopes offered a new, detailed view of the Black Eye Galaxy, showcasing advancements in astronomical observation. [cite: Space.com]  
  
🔬🧬 **HIV-Like Virus Cleared in Infant Primates**. 🔬 Researchers successfully used a triple-dose antibody regimen to eliminate an HIV-like virus in newborn primates, a promising breakthrough that could accelerate human trials for an effective treatment. [cite: AI News in a Minute]  
  
⚛️🧪 **World's First Superconducting Quantum Heat Engine Demonstrated**. 🧪 Scientists demonstrated the first cyclic quantum heat engine, a miniature superconducting device capable of converting heat near absolute zero into usable work, opening new avenues in quantum thermodynamics. [cite: ScienceDaily]
