## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🤖 Q: What is high conflict?  
  
🤖 A: High conflict is a destructive form of feud that escalates to the point of becoming conflict for conflict's sake, trapping participants in binary thinking and harming the core interests they originally sought to protect.  
  
### 🔄 Q: How does the looping technique work in communication?  
  
🔄 A: Looping is a four-step listening method where a person listens for what is most important to the speaker, summarizes the message in their own words, asks the speaker to confirm accuracy, and continues iterating until the speaker feels fully understood.  
  
### 🚨 Q: What is the normalcy bias during a disaster?  
  
🚨 A: Normalcy bias is a psychological reflex where the brain interprets sudden dangerous cues through the lens of past routine, leading people to delay evacuation, freeze, or carry on normal activities despite immediate threats.  
  
### 🗞️ Q: Why are people increasingly avoiding the news?  
  
🗞️ A: People avoid the news because constant exposure to aerosolized, fear-driven reporting triggers anxiety and helpless despair by focusing on outrage while omitting actionable solutions and constructive agency.  
  
### 🔀 Q: What is political splitting?  
  
🔀 A: Splitting is a psychological tendency to divide complex societies into simplified good-versus-evil camps, which causes people to miscalculate their opponents' beliefs and overlook shared values.
