## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)  
  
### 🤔 Q: How does the House Oversight Committee plan to prevent a high volume of corruption stories from overwhelming the public?  
  
💡 A: The House Oversight Committee focuses on four primary areas - the Trump family, DHS and ICE pay-to-play and policies, corporate corruption including Venezuela oil deals and surveillance pricing, and the Jeffrey Epstein investigation - while prioritizing inquiries with a legislative purpose and clear communication tied to affordability \[[23:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IFFUOrW30rc&t=1414)].  
  
### ⚖️ Q: What tools does a congressional majority have when facing executive branch stonewalling and Department of Justice resistance?  
  
💡 A: Being in the majority allows the House to go to court on behalf of the chamber, pursue contempt referrals through the court system rather than relying on the Department of Justice, coordinate with other committees, and work alongside state attorneys general \[[08:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IFFUOrW30rc&t=535)].  
  
### 📂 Q: What remains to be investigated regarding the Jeffrey Epstein files and law enforcement handling?  
  
💡 A: Oversight focuses on the roughly two and a half million unreleased documents at the Department of Justice, protecting survivors, examining why prior and current officials limited or interfered with the investigation, and reforming how law enforcement handles sex trafficking \[[44:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IFFUOrW30rc&t=2673)].  
  
### 📚 Q: Which books are recommended at the end of the interview?
